# SC26 BoF: HPC User Support in the AI and Exascale Era


##  Information for Participants

### When and where
- **Time:** Thursday, 19 November 2026 12:15pm - 1:15pm CST
- **Location:** W180

### What this session is about
As HPC enters an era of extreme heterogeneity and AI integration, user support is a critical strategic enabler. However, rising system complexity and finite staffing strain traditional models. To thrive in the AI and exascale era, a collaborative community approach is essential.

This BoF session invites practitioners, providers, and users to collaboratively reshape HPC support. Featuring insights from facility leaders on managing rapid AI adoption, an interactive live survey, and open group discussions, participants will share experiences regarding scaling, AI automation, and knowledge transfer. Outcomes will form a post-conference report dedicated to fostering a sustainable, community-driven support ecosystem.

### Why join?
This session is a chance to:
- meet others working on similar support challenges
- share practical experiences and pain points
- discuss how AI, automation, and knowledge sharing can help
- contribute ideas for stronger collaboration across organisations
- help shape future community-driven support practices

### Who should attend?
Whether you work in public research or industry, and whether you provide support or receive it, we'd be happy to have you join. This session is about learning from each other and building a more sustainable future for HPC user support together.

## How to participate
Please come ready to:
- share examples from your own environment
- discuss challenges openly
- listen to others' approaches
- help identify practical ideas for follow-up after the conference

No preparation is required.

## Session structure and setup
This is an interactive session with minimal formal presentation.

### Structure
1. **Introduction** – overview of goals, themes, and outcomes  
2. **Live survey** – quick interactive survey to understand who is in the room and what challenges matter most  
3. **Roundtable discussions** – small-group discussions focused on practical experiences and ideas  
4. **Report back** – each table shares key takeaways  
5. **Prioritisation and conclusion** – participants vote on promising ideas and discuss next steps

### Session setup
Tables will include:
- a discussion handouts
- markers
- voting stickers

We aim to leave the session with:
- a shortlist of promising ideas and shared practices
- documented insights from the discussions
- a starting point for a broader international HPC user support community
- follow-up material shared after the conference, likely as a **white paper or blog post**
